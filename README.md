# World News — Tuesday 24 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 24 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124).

A 1024×1024 newspaper front page summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026:
the overnight Russian strike on a residential block in Zaporizhzhia, the
AI stock rally after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang, Moscow's warning to
Estonia, the U.S. holding back Patriot missiles for Ukraine, and the
magnitude 5.6 earthquake in Nepal — plus the day's other news and sport.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`, after checking that it is a whole PNG of at most
1 MiB. Run it twice and both runs write the same bytes.

## Stories in the image

- **Lead — Zaporizhzhia:** a Russian attack overnight partly destroyed a
  five-storey residential building in the Kosmichnyi district; two people
  were killed and 22 wounded, including two children (Reuters).
- **Markets — Huang is back:** Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as chief
  executive on Monday; shares jumped 31% and nearly $1 trillion in value,
  and Asian markets surged on Tuesday — Seoul up 7.4%, Tokyo up 5%
  (Reuters, CNN).
- **NATO — Estonia:** Moscow warned the NATO ally it was "on verge of
  open war" after Tallinn said it would shut its border crossings with
  Russia from 1 December; Estonia denies letting Ukraine use its airspace
  (Reuters, The Independent).
- **United States — Patriots:** the Pentagon has yet to approve about 60
  interceptors Washington had agreed to send Ukraine, the Financial Times
  reports; Kyiv says it urgently needs them.
- **Asia — Nepal quake:** a magnitude 5.6 tremor struck Taplejung near
  the Tibet border on Tuesday morning; shaking was felt in India, Bhutan
  and Bangladesh, with no casualties reported so far.
- **Also today:** fourteen nations affirm the ICJ's genocide ruling
  against Myanmar over the Rohingya; Israeli strikes in Gaza kill three,
  including a UNRWA worker; Germany takes reports of a Russian
  nuclear-armed satellite seriously; China opens its Tiangong station to
  its first foreign astronaut, from Pakistan.
- **Sport:** the Trail Blazers end the 76ers' five-game win streak,
  128–111; the Pacers edge the Thunder 125–120 as Shai Gilgeous-Alexander
  scores 38 on his return.

The hero illustration is an original drawing of rescue workers at the
damaged Zaporizhzhia block; all headlines and text are set in the page
itself, so the image reads on its own. Every story above happened, or
was first reported, on Tuesday 24 November 2026.
